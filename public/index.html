Practical daily actions

  • Combine errands and avoid unnecessary idling.
  • Prefer public transport or carpooling for suitable trips.
  • Check trusted local readings before prolonged outdoor exercise.
  • Ventilate when outdoor air is cleaner than indoor air.
  • Use a correctly sized HEPA purifier; avoid indoor combustion sources.
